Methodology — model radar-2

The formula

implied = TVL × category multiple × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier × chains multiplier

  • Category multiple. Median FDV/TVL of the live-token protocols in the same category, measured on this snapshot. Needs at least 3 live protocols in the category; otherwise the global median is used. Clamped to 0.08 – 1.00.
  • Growth multiplier. 1 + (clamped 7-day change ÷ 100) × 0.6. The 7-day change is clamped to −60% … +120% first, so one hot week cannot dominate the score.
  • Maturity multiplier. 0.65 + min(age in days, 1095) ÷ 1095 × 0.35. Older protocols score higher, up to 1.00 at three years. Unknown age is treated as 365 days.
  • Chains multiplier. 1 + 0.02 × min(number of chains, 5). At most +10%.
  • Base. TVL × category multiple. The final value is rounded to whole dollars, minimum $1.

Honest limits

  • One snapshot, dated . Prices, TVL and 7-day change move daily; this board is stale the moment the snapshot is taken.
  • Implied value is a model claim, not a price and not a prediction of when a token launches — or whether one ever does.
  • "Records no token" describes the DefiLlama record, not the protocol. A listed protocol may still hold a token its record does not carry.
  • The farm plan is a generic seven-step routine derived from category, chains, TVL and age. It is not advice, and farming costs gas and carries risk.
  • Only protocols the snapshot carries can be ranked. Anything missing from the snapshot is missing from the board.
